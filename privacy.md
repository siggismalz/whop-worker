# Privacy Policy for Whop Worker

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Whop Worker is a private automation tool used to prepare and publish authorized short-form video content to connected social media accounts.

## Information We Process

Whop Worker may process information provided by connected platforms, including account identifiers, authorization tokens, basic account information, video publishing information, and publishing status.

## How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide the functionality of Whop Worker, including:

- Connecting authorized social media accounts
- Uploading and publishing authorized video content
- Checking publishing status
- Preventing duplicate uploads
- Managing content publishing workflows

Whop Worker does not sell personal information.

## TikTok Data

When a TikTok account is connected, Whop Worker may access TikTok data only within the permissions explicitly authorized by the user through TikTok's authorization process.

TikTok authorization tokens and related account information are used only to provide the requested publishing functionality.

## Data Storage and Security

Authentication credentials and authorization tokens are stored securely and are not intentionally made publicly available.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the service or maintain required publishing records.

Users may request deletion of their data or revoke Whop Worker's access through the relevant social media platform.

## Third-Party Services

Whop Worker may interact with third-party services such as TikTok, YouTube, and other platforms. Use of those services is also subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.
